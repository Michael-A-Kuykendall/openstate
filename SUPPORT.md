## What makes a useful report?

Search existing issues first. Include your OpenState and host versions, operating system, expected and actual behavior, and the smallest repeatable example. A synthetic example is enough; do not upload your project or complete session transcripts. Remove keys, tokens, purchase email addresses, passwords and private paths from errors and screenshots.

Maintainers review reports, may ask for a smaller reproduction, and label or link duplicates. An issue records a problem; it does not promise a fix date. Follow up in the same issue when requested information is available. If an issue is closed as a duplicate, continue on the linked report.

Questions and early feature ideas belong in Discussions. When an idea has agreed scope, a maintainer can open an implementation issue and link the discussion.

## Contributing and conduct

Everyone can report issues and participate in Discussions under the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Pull requests are limited to approved maintainers; read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before preparing one.
