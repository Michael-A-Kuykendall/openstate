# License and account FAQ

**Lost your key? [Recover your license](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/recover).**

[View keys and machines](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/account) · [Manage billing or cancel](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/portal) · [Request purchase help](mailto:mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev)

Keep license keys, purchase emails, receipts and payment details out of public issues and Discussions.

## How do I recover my license key?

1. Open [Recover a key](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/recover) and enter the email used at checkout.
2. Keygen emails you a link to set a password. Follow that link, then [sign in to your licenses](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/account) with your purchase email and password.
3. Copy your key and store it securely, for example in your password manager.

For privacy, the recovery page gives the same response whether or not the address has a purchase. Check spam and that you used the purchase email. The receipt and license key are different: keep both.

If recovery still fails or you no longer have access to that email account, email [mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev](mailto:mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev). Explain the problem privately and include a receipt or order reference if available. Do not send card numbers, passwords or your full license key. Account access may need to be verified before help can be provided.

## Do I need an account to install or activate?

No account is needed to install or activate with your key. Account sign-in is for retrieving keys and managing machines. Use the activation instructions on the [license page](https://michael-a-kuykendall.github.io/openstate/license.html#activate). Save your license key before closing the purchase confirmation page.

## Where can I see my keys or remove an old machine?

[Your licenses](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/account) lists your keys and machines after sign-in. One paid license covers one developer on up to 3 machines. Remove a machine you no longer use there, or run `openstate-engine license deactivate` on the old machine before activating the new one. A license is not transferable to another person.

## How do I cancel, change my card or get an invoice?

Open [Manage billing](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/portal) and enter your license key. This opens Stripe's billing portal for your purchase, where you can cancel, update payment details or download invoices. An email address alone cannot open billing. If you lost the key, recover it first.

Cancellation stops future monthly renewals; your monthly license remains usable until the end of the paid period. Cancellation does not refund earlier charges.

## Does the lifetime license expire?

The $129 lifetime license has no scheduled subscription expiry and covers **every 0.x release**. It is not a subscription and does not need monthly renewal. It does **not** include a promise of future 1.x or later major versions. See the [license terms](https://michael-a-kuykendall.github.io/openstate/terms.html) for the scope of the license.

The engine still checks a paid license periodically and can work offline for up to **7 days** between checks. An expired offline verification window is different from the lifetime entitlement expiring: reconnect so the license can be checked.

## Is the free trial limited to a number of days?

The trial provides **30 successful structural applies per machine**, not 30 days. Searching, reading and previews remain free. See [pricing](https://michael-a-kuykendall.github.io/openstate/pricing.html) for current plans.

## Can I switch from monthly to lifetime?

[Switch to lifetime](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/upgrade) using your monthly license key. You pay $129 once for every 0.x release, keep the same key and registered machines, and the monthly subscription stops. There is no credit for the unused part of the paid month.

## How do I request a refund?

There is no advertised automatic refund window. The published policy is **all sales are final**, with requests reviewed individually; approval is not guaranteed.

Email [mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev](mailto:mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev) from your purchase email with a receipt or order reference and a brief explanation. The maintainer reviews the circumstances privately. Do not post purchase details in GitHub issues or Discussions.

See the [refund policy](https://michael-a-kuykendall.github.io/openstate/refund.html) and [terms](https://michael-a-kuykendall.github.io/openstate/terms.html). To stop future monthly charges, [cancel in billing](https://openstate-license.michaelallenkuykendall.workers.dev/portal) as well; a refund request is not a cancellation.
