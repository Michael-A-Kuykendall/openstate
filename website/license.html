License

Buy, copy your key, activate it.

There is no account to create. You pay through Stripe, the next page shows your license key, and one command turns it on for the machine you are on.

After you buy

  1. Pay on Stripe’s checkout page, monthly or lifetime. You confirm there that the purchase is final.
  2. Copy the key from the page Stripe returns you to. Keep it somewhere safe; it is tied to your purchase email, which can recover it.
  3. Activate it on each machine: openstate-engine license activate <key>, or install.sh --license <key> when installing.

Machines and offline use

  • One license covers you on up to 3 machines. To move it, run openstate-engine license deactivate on the old machine first. If the old machine is gone, remove it from your licenses.
  • The engine checks the license and then works offline for up to 7 days between checks.
  • Monthly licenses renew with each payment; a cancelled subscription keeps working to the end of the paid month.

Cancel or update billing

Open billing, paste your license key, and Stripe’s billing page lets you cancel, change your card or download invoices. Your key is the login; an email address alone cannot open it.

On monthly and want lifetime? Switch to lifetime with your key: you pay the lifetime price once, keep the same key, and the subscription stops.

Lost your key?

Recover it with your purchase email: Keygen emails you a link to set a password, and signing in shows your keys. The reply is the same whether or not an email bought a license. Never post a license key or purchase email in a public issue.

Licenses are issued by Keygen; payments are handled by Stripe. What the engine sends when it checks a license is in the privacy policy.
