Frequently asked questions

Does lifetime expire?

The $129 lifetime license has no scheduled subscription expiry and covers every 0.x release. It does not promise coverage of future 1.x or later major versions. The engine still needs a license check at least every 7 days for continued offline use; that verification window is separate from your lifetime entitlement. See the terms.

How do I cancel or request a refund?

Cancel monthly billing with your key to stop future renewals. Access continues until the end of your paid period. A cancellation does not refund previous charges. Refund requests are reviewed individually under the final-sale policy; approval is not guaranteed. Email mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev from your purchase email with a receipt or order reference and a brief explanation.

What if recovery does not work?

Check spam and that you used the purchase email. If you cannot access that email account or still cannot recover your key, contact purchase support privately. Never share your key, receipt or purchase email in a public issue. The full FAQ covers recovery, account sign-in, machine removal and upgrades.

Stripe hosts the payment

The license worker creates a Stripe Checkout Session and redirects the browser to it. After payment, Stripe returns you to the worker's success page, which shows your key. Save your license key before closing that page. Copy it into your password manager or another secure place. Your Stripe receipt is proof of payment; keep the key separately for activation.

What happens after checkout

  1. Pay on Stripe’s checkout page, monthly or lifetime (every 0.x release). You confirm there that the purchase is final.
  2. Copy the key from the page Stripe returns you to. Keep it somewhere safe; it is tied to your purchase email, which can recover it.
  3. Activate it on each machine: openstate-engine license activate <key>, or install.sh --license <key> when installing.

Machines, transfer and offline use

  • One license covers you on up to 3 machines. To transfer it to a new machine, run openstate-engine license deactivate on the old machine first. If the old machine is gone, remove it from your licenses.
  • The engine checks the license and then works offline for up to 7 days between checks.
  • Monthly licenses renew with each payment; a cancelled subscription keeps working to the end of the paid month.

Cancel or update billing

Open billing, paste your license key, and Stripe’s billing page lets you cancel, change your card or download invoices. Your key is the login; an email address alone cannot open it.

On monthly and want lifetime (every 0.x release)? Switch to Lifetime (every 0.x release) with your key: you pay $129 once for every 0.x release, keep the same key, and the subscription stops.

Need purchase support or want to request a refund? Email mkuykendall@deltazerolabs.dev from your purchase email. Requests are reviewed under our final-sale policy; approval is not guaranteed.

Lost your key?

Recover it with your purchase email: Keygen emails you a link to set a password, and signing in shows your keys. The reply is the same whether or not an email bought a license. Never post a license key or purchase email in a public issue.

Licenses are issued by Keygen; payments are handled by Stripe. What the engine sends when it checks a license is in the privacy policy.
