What you get

  • Free use: anyone may install and use OpenState; on each machine the first 30 structural applies are free.

  • Personal and commercial work are both covered.
  • Lifetime ($129): every 0.x release. Monthly ($10/month): while the subscription is paid; a cancelled subscription runs to the end of the paid period. A monthly license can be switched to lifetime by paying the lifetime price; the key stays the same and the unused part of the month is not credited. How the license works

Stripe takes the payment; Keygen issues a license key, shown on the success page. Activate it with openstate-engine license activate <key> or install.sh --license <key>. The engine checks licenses over the network: on the free tier it counts each structural apply with Keygen; a paid license is verified and works offline for up to 7 days between checks. It sends the license key, a hashed machine fingerprint and the platform name, never project data. Circumventing the check is not permitted by these terms.
