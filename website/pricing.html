Free to try. $10 a month or $129 once for every 0.x release.

Every machine gets the whole product free, including 30 structural applies. When those are used, a license removes the limit: $10 a month, or $129 once for every 0.x release. Checkout is hosted by Stripe. All sales are final. Try it free first.

Machines and offline use

  • One license covers one named developer on up to 3 machines. To move it to a new machine, run openstate-engine license deactivate on the old one first; if the old machine is gone, remove it from your licenses page.
  • The engine checks the license and then works offline for up to 7 days between checks.
  • A cancelled monthly subscription keeps working to the end of the paid period. Monthly can switch to the $129 one-time plan later at full price, keeping the same key.

Stripe returns you to the license worker’s success page, which shows your key and the command to activate it: openstate-engine license activate <key>. Project state stays local; it is not uploaded to complete the sale.
